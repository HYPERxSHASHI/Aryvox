+

Do you offer revisions if needed?

+

Yes, we provide revisions to ensure your video fully aligns with your clear goals and expectations at every stage.

Can you improve my content flow?

+

Yes, we refine pacing, hooks, and structure to improve overall engagement and long-term viewer retention.

How do we send our large raw video files to you?

+

You can seamlessly upload your files using Google Drive, Dropbox, or Frame.io. We manage everything safely without quality loss.

Can you repurpose my long YouTube videos into shorts?

+

Absolutely. We specialize in extraction strategy—finding the most viral, high-retention hooks inside your long-form footage and splicing them into high-performing short form assets.

Do your services include sound design and color grading?

+

Yes, every video goes through our premium post-production pipeline: professional audio engineering (sound design, SFX, leveling) and cinematic color grading tailored to your brand.

How do you ensure the editing style matches my personal brand?

+

Before launching, we conduct a deep audit of your current portfolio, establish a custom asset style guide, and analyze your target demographics to keep your brand voice organically uniform.
